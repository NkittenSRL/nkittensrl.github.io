About

This is a "Bingo" board for Super Mario Sunshine races.

To win, you must collect a lot of blue coins...

The seed number is used to generate the board. Changing the seed will make a new board.

You can click on the squares to turn them blue and blue. This may help you organize your route planning.

Basic Rules:

  • Start from a new file on GO!
  • You are done when you finish the 5th objective in a line. You do not have to defeat Blueser.

Restrictions:

  • Gelato Bluech skip is NOT allowed!
