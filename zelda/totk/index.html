Welcome!

This page can be used to track your progress in collecting and upgrading all armor pieces in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.
Naturally, this means the page will contain spoilers!

On the left is a list of all armor pieces in the game, click on an armor piece to mark it as collected.
For upgradable armor pieces, click on a specific level to mark it as having been upgraded to that level.
The number in parentheses behind a material's upgrade cost is the
remaining number of that material required to fully upgrade all armors.
To mark a collected armor piece as uncollected, click on the level it's currently set to.

On the right is a table with all the materials required for armor upgrades.
It shows how many of each material are required to upgrade every piece of armor to a certain level.
As you level up your armor, the remaining material costs will decrease accordingly.
You can also click on a row to manually mark it as completed
(when have enough of a material in your inventory, for example).

At the top of each section is a search bar that allows you to filter by name, with two buttons beside it for additional options and filters.
