Goal:

Complete five of the tasks in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Standard bingo rules apply

  • Win all 3 Market minigames (no Sun's Song)
    • Clear the Shooting Gallery, Treasure Box Shop, and Bombchu Bowling (any prize)
    • Using Sun's Song in Treasure Box Shop is not allowed
  • Launch 13 Gossip Stones with Bombs
    • Using Bombchu does not count
  • 3 Shields + 3 filled bottle slots
    • Bottle slots = bottom left part of Select Item screen
  • Look at Mario's portrait
    • Find the portrait of Mario in Hyrule Castle
  • Megaton Hammer (no damage in Fire)
    • Reset immediately if you take any damage in Fire Temple
  • Sink with the boat in Shadow Temple
    • Void out while standing on the deck of the ferry
  • 5 small keys in GTG (no duping, no damage)
    • Keys may not be duped
    • Reset if you take any damage while in GTG
  • JOTWAL
    • Jump off the watchtower and live (get revived by a fairy)
