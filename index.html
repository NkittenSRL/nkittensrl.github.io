Splatoon Tools

Randomizer and Elimination Mode tools for use in Private Battles in Splatoon 1, 2, and 3,
as well as pages listing all weapons for each game, and a Freshness tracker for Splatoon 3.

Super Mario Sunshine Bingo

An archive of all versions of SMS bingo I had any involvement with.
I created the first version of SMS bingo, and hosted the second and third versions, created by icedwindowsil, on my site as well.

I also made a proof of concept page for an idea I had: Square flip bingo, where each goal reveals a second goal behind it when completed.

Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom armor tracker

A tool to track your progress in collecting and upgrading all armor pieces in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.
Also lists the upgrade costs and calculates the remaining materials you need to upgrade everything.
